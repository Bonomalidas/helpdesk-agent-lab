---
title: Account Locked After Failed Sign-ins
topic: account-lockout
updated: 2025-11-05
---
Accounts lock automatically after five failed sign-in attempts. A lock clears by itself after 30 minutes, or immediately if you use the self-service unlock page or call the IT Service Desk with your employee ID. Repeated lockouts are usually caused by an old saved password on your phone mail app or a mapped drive. Remove the saved password from every device, then sign in again with your current password.
