---
title: Submit Expenses in Workday
topic: expenses
updated: 2025-10-02
---
Sign in to Workday from your Okta dashboard to submit expenses. Create a new expense report, add each item with a category, and attach receipts for anything over 25 dollars. Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Your manager approves the report and reimbursement arrives in the next payroll cycle. If the Workday tile is missing from Okta, open a ticket and mention your employee ID.
