---
title: Replace Your Laptop
topic: hardware
updated: 2025-12-12
---
Laptops are eligible for replacement after four years or earlier when there is a confirmed hardware fault. Open a Hardware Request in the catalog and describe the problem. Before pickup, make sure your files are synced to OneDrive because the old device is wiped. Collect the new laptop from the IT desk on your floor with your badge. Return the old laptop and charger within five business days.
