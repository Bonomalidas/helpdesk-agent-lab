---
title: Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication (Okta Verify)
topic: mfa
updated: 2026-02-11
---
All employees must enroll in multi-factor authentication with Okta Verify. Install Okta Verify from your phone app store, then sign in to your Okta account settings and scan the QR code shown on screen. Turn on push notifications and add a backup SMS number. If you lose your phone, the IT Service Desk can reset your factors after your manager approves the request. Never approve a push notification you did not trigger yourself.
