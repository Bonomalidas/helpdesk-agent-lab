---
title: New Hire Equipment and First Day Checklist
topic: onboarding
updated: 2026-01-30
---
New hire laptops ship to your home address five days before your start date. You may request an external monitor and keyboard from the equipment form. On your first day, turn on the laptop, connect to the internet, and sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email. Then activate Okta Verify and set your permanent password. Contact the Service Desk if the laptop has not arrived two days before your start date.
