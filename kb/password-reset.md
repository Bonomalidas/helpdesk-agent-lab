---
title: Reset Your Password
topic: password
updated: 2026-01-20
---
If you forgot your password, use the self-service portal at password.acme.example. Click Forgot password and verify your identity with an Okta Verify push or an SMS code. Choose a new password that is at least 14 characters long and that you have not used in your last 10 passwords. The new password syncs to email and your laptop within 15 minutes, so lock and unlock your laptop while connected to the network to pick it up. If your account is locked after several failed attempts, see the Account Locked article instead.
