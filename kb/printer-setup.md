---
title: Add a Network Printer
topic: printing
updated: 2025-10-14
---
Printers are named by floor, for example PRN-3F-East. Open Self Service, search for the printer name and click Add. Release your print job by tapping your badge on the printer. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours. If the printer shows offline, restart the print spooler by signing out and back in, then try again before contacting the Service Desk.
