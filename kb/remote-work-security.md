---
title: Security Rules for Working Remotely
topic: security
updated: 2025-12-01
---
When working from a cafe or other public place, always connect through the corporate VPN and lock your screen when you step away. Do not plug personal USB drives into company laptops. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within one hour so it can be locked remotely. Never share your password or approve an unexpected multi-factor prompt.
