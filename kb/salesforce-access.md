title: Request Salesforce Access
topic: salesforce
updated: 2026-02-24
---
Request Salesforce access in the Access Portal and choose the role you need, either Sales Rep or Sales Manager. Your manager approves the request and you must have completed the Salesforce basics training first. After approval, access is provisioned through Okta within four business hours. The Salesforce tile then appears on your Okta dashboard. If the tile is missing after a day, open a ticket with your request number.
