---
title: Get Access to a Shared Mailbox
topic: email
updated: 2025-08-19
---
To get access to a shared mailbox, open a ticket with the mailbox address and the reason you need it. The mailbox owner or your manager must approve the request. Once approved, the mailbox appears in Outlook within one hour; restart Outlook if it does not show up. You can send as the shared mailbox only if the request specifically includes send-as permission.
