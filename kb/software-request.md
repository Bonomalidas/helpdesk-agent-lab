title: Request New Software
topic: software
updated: 2026-01-08
---
Standard applications such as the browser, office suite and chat tools install automatically from the Self Service catalog. For licensed software, open the Software Request form in the catalog, enter a business reason and your cost center, and submit it for manager approval. Approved requests are fulfilled in about two business days. Software that is not in the catalog needs a security review first, which can take up to two weeks.
