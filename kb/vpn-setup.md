---
title: Connect to the Corporate VPN (GlobalProtect)
topic: vpn
updated: 2026-03-02
---
To work remotely you must connect to the corporate VPN using the GlobalProtect client. Step 1: Install GlobalProtect from the Self Service portal. Step 2: Open GlobalProtect and enter the portal address vpn.acme.example. Step 3: Sign in with your company email and approve the Okta Verify push notification. Step 4: Wait for the status to show Connected. If the connection drops repeatedly, switch from Wi-Fi to a wired network and reconnect. If you see the error Gateway unreachable, check that your laptop clock is correct and contact the IT Service Desk. Do not use personal VPN software on company laptops.
