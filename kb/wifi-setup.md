---
title: Connect to Office Wi-Fi
topic: wifi
updated: 2025-09-30
---
Company laptops connect to the Corp-WiFi network automatically once the device is enrolled in management. Sign in with your company email and password when prompted. Visitors should use the Guest-WiFi network and ask reception for the daily access code. Personal phones can use Guest-WiFi only. If Corp-WiFi keeps asking for credentials, forget the network on your laptop and reconnect so the enrollment certificate is refreshed.
