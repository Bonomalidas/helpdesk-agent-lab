---
title: Request a Zoom License
topic: zoom
updated: 2025-09-12
---
Every employee can join Zoom meetings with a free account. People managers can request a Zoom Pro license from the Software catalog, which allows meetings longer than 40 minutes. A webinar license needs approval from your director and a business reason. Licenses are reviewed every quarter and removed if the account has not hosted a meeting in 90 days.
