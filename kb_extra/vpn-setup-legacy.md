---
title: Connect to the VPN from Home (Cisco AnyConnect)
topic: vpn
updated: 2022-03-14
---
To connect to the VPN from home, open Cisco AnyConnect and connect to vpn-old.acme.example. Enter your network username and password, then your RSA token code. The VPN client is installed by default on all laptops. Once the VPN shows connected you can reach the intranet and file shares from home. If you cannot connect to the VPN from home, restart AnyConnect and try the VPN again. Home users should always keep the VPN connected while working.
